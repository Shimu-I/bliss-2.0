# User Manual

Open the site and choose **Sign in**. Where you land depends on your role.
If you are a new parent, choose **Register** first.

## Parents
**Create an account**: Register with your name, email, phone and a password of 8+ characters.

**Register your child**: open **Children**, fill in name, date of birth, gender, special needs and any medical
history (allergies, asthma…), then **Register child**. A caregiver is assigned automatically.

**Follow your child's day**
- **Overview**: a card per child with today's attendance and their caregiver.
- **Activity**: a timeline of attendance, activities, meals, learning notes and pick-up / drop-off.
- **Inbox**: messages from the centre. Unread count shows on the tab; use **Mark all read**.

**Bills**: open **Bills** to see amount, due date and status (Pending, Paid, Overdue).
Pay at the centre; staff will mark the bill as paid and you will get a message.

**Remove a child**: **Children** > **Remove** (this also deletes their records).

## Caregivers
Your account is created by the admin (they give you a temporary password).
- **My children**: one card per assigned child. Tap **Present** or **Absent**; tapping again corrects it.
- **Records**: pick a child and a type (activity, learning note, meal, pick-up, drop-off), add a title and details,
  then **Add record**. For meals, put allergy notes in *Details*.
- Parents are notified for every attendance mark and record, so keep entries short and kind.

## Admin
- **Overview**: totals for children, caregivers, parents, present today and outstanding money.
- **Children**: see everyone, change a child's caregiver from the dropdown (then **Save**), or remove a child.
- **Caregivers**: **Add caregiver** (name, email, temporary password, qualification, experience, training).
  Removing a caregiver un-assigns their children; reassign them under Children.
- **Parents**: read-only list with number of children.
- **Payments**: create a bill (parent, type, amount, due date), and **Mark paid** when money is received.

**First-day checklist**: add caregivers, wait for parents to register, check every child has a caregiver.

## FAQ
- **I forgot my password.** Ask the admin. Password reset by email is not available yet.
- **Wrong attendance?** The caregiver taps the correct button; it replaces the earlier entry for the day.
- **Too many login attempts.** Wait one minute and try again.
- **Is my data private?** Parents only see their own children; caregivers only see their assigned children.
